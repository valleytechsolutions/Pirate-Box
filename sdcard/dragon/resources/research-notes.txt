Dragon Detector: interpreting radio evidence

Registry assignments identify address registrants, not finished products or operators.
MA-S (36 bits) takes precedence over MA-M (28), then MA-L (24).
Random/private BLE addresses and locally administered Wi-Fi addresses have no OUI claim.
Bluetooth Company Identifiers and IEEE address registrants are different namespaces.
Advertising a service UUID is not proof that every associated GATT characteristic exists.
Preserve raw bytes and address type with an observation; record unknowns rather than guess.
Use existing BLE Lab/DDG1 profiles for owned-device service inspection and comparison.
Notification payloads require a known format; unknown values remain hexadecimal.
Observed advertisements can be spoofed; a label alone is not detection confidence.
No executable rules or scripts are loaded from this pack.
